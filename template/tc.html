Terms and Conditions

Guidelines for online bookings and temple visits

1. Identification & Entry

Devotees must carry their original Government ID (Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID) entered during reservation. Passes are non-transferable.

2. Traditional Dress Code

Traditional Indian attire is mandatory. Men: Dhoti/Kurta. Women: Saree or Chudidar with Dupatta.

3. Cancellation & Refunds

Cancellations made at least 48 hours in advance are eligible for full refund back to original payment mode within 3 business days.

4. Electronic Devices

Mobile phones and cameras are prohibited inside the sanctum. Free cloakroom facilities are available at entry gates.
